# FFXI 75-era canon primer (for squad backstories and party chat)

This is the truth for the squad-chat sidecar. If a detail is not here and you are not certain,
keep it vague rather than inventing canon. The server is the 75-era: base game + Rise of the
Zilart + Chains of Promathia + Treasures of Aht Urhgan. Do not use Wings of the Goddess,
Abyssea, Seekers of Adoulin, Rhapsodies, or later content.

## The world

- Vana'diel. The present is about twenty years after the Crystal War.
- The three great nations are San d'Oria, Bastok, and Windurst. Jeuno is the neutral trade city
  that brokered their alliance during the war.
- The Beastmen include Orcs, Yagudo, Quadav, Goblins, and others. The Shadow Lord united them
  into the Beastman Confederate during the Crystal War.
- Magic is real and part of daily life. Death is real. The war is recent enough that many adults
  remember it, lost someone in it, or fought in it.
- Adventurers are a normal profession. The nations hire independent adventurers for problems
  their own armies cannot or will not handle.

## The five races

### Hume
- Originated in the Bastok region and spread across Vana'diel.
- Adaptable, commercial, balanced. Both sexes are common.
- Bastok is their great achievement: mining, metallurgy, engineering, firearms.
- No reliable canon lifespan figure; do not invent one.

### Elvaan
- Most strongly associated with San d'Oria.
- Proud, traditional, religious, skilled with swords. Both sexes are present.
- San d'Orian society is closed and wary of outsiders; individual Elvaan range from genuinely
  noble to condescending.
- No reliable canon lifespan figure; do not invent one.

### Tarutaru
- Associated with Windurst and its magic-centered society.
- Small and childlike in stature, but adults; their appearance does not indicate their age.
- Accomplished scholars and mages. Both sexes are present in government and society.
- Their long friendship with the Mithra is important to Windurst.
- Never write a Tarutaru as a human child.

### Galka
- Their old capital was in the Altepa Desert on Zepwell Island. It fell to ant-like beastmen
  roughly six centuries before the present, and many Galka eventually settled in Bastok.
- Physically powerful; heavily represented in Bastok's mines and military, but poor and
  marginalized. The Hume-Galka tension in Bastok is the clearest institutional prejudice in
  the setting.
- Conventionally referred to as male, but Galka have no specific gender and do not reproduce
  sexually. Their belief is reincarnation: a Galka who dies returns as a new Galka child. The
  exact mechanism is not settled; one in-game account presents it as something that may be
  folklore. Use it as a personal belief, not a hard rule.
- No reliable canon lifespan figure; do not invent one.

### Mithra
- Predominantly female hunters. Their homeland is Gha Naboh, far to the south on Olzhirya,
  outside the playable map. Kazham, in the Elshimo jungles, is a well-known Mithra/Tarutaru
  settlement.
- Males are very rare and generally stay home; only female Mithra commonly venture out.
  Do not invent a precise male/female ratio or detailed reproductive customs.
- Many Mithra live and work in Windurst; the two peoples are close allies.
- Often described as energetic, curious, and mischievous; that is a stereotype, not every
  individual.

## The three nations

### San d'Oria
- An Elvaan kingdom ruled by the d'Oraguille royal line.
- The Royal Knights patrol the borders and conduct punitive expeditions. The Temple Knights,
  funded by the Cathedral, keep domestic order and serve religious functions.
- Culture prizes knighthood, tradition, fine blades, and public honor. Agriculture is rich, but
  taxes and long military service weigh on commoners.
- Historically expansionist; lost power after Bastok's victory at Konschtat and a period of
  internal turmoil. Still proud, still poorer than its ceremonies suggest.
- Socially closed. A non-Elvaan can serve, trade, or live there, but is always reminded that
  they are an outsider.

### Bastok
- A Hume-founded republic with a president and a senate. Mining, metallurgy, engineering, and
  firearms define its material culture.
- Its army is an organized citizen militia with legions; elite units include the Iron Musketeers
  and the Mythril Musketeers.
- Prosperous but crowded, dependent on food imports, and divided by serious Hume-Galka
  inequality. Many Galka do the dangerous work and live in poverty.

### Windurst
- A federation of Tarutaru tribes governed by five ministries and a council of representatives.
  The Star Sybil's prophecies guide national decisions.
- Magic is used in farming and daily life; the culture values learning and is comparatively
  non-materialistic. The War Warlocks are its magical military core, supported by Cardians.
- Mithra serve in mercenary brigades rather than a fully regimented force. Windurst was badly
  damaged in the Crystal War and rebuilt with Mithra aid.
- Long conflict and uneasy accommodation with the Yagudo.

### Jeuno
- Neutral trade city, wealthy and cosmopolitan. Its leader Kam'lanaut persuaded the three
  nations to ally during the Crystal War. The alliance did not erase their mutual suspicion.

## The Crystal War

- About twenty years before the present. A commonly used chronology places it in 862-864 C.E.;
  the exact present year is not important for personal stories.
- The Shadow Lord appeared suddenly, gathered the Beastmen into the Beastman Confederate, and
  attacked the nations. They initially fought separately and suffered defeats.
- After Jeuno brokered the alliance, the Allies held at Jeuno, won a costly victory at
  Xarcabard, and stormed Castle Zvahl. Volker delivered the final blow, according to the
  official history.
- The victory was devastating. Cities were ruined, many died, and the nations remained
  distrustful afterward. They prefer to hire adventurers for ongoing threats.
- Base-game mission spoiler: the Shadow Lord is revealed to be Raogrimm, the Galka Talekeeper.
  Do not put this in casual squad chat unless the player has already reached that story.

## The era beyond the war

- Rise of the Zilart: the ruins of the Zilart civilization, Tu'Lia, and the Sky. Ancient
  technology and the Arks. A story about the past reaching into the present.
- Chains of Promathia: the Emptiness, the Tavnazian Archipelago, and the legacy of Promathia.
  Darker and more personal than the nation missions.
- Treasures of Aht Urhgan: the Aht Urhgan Empire, the Near East, the Mamool Ja, and the
  Imperial capital. A foreign power with its own problems.
- The squad does not need to be involved in these storylines. They are adventurers, not the
  chosen ones. Keep their personal stories small enough to fit beside the player's.

## Jobs in this era

### Warrior (WAR)
- Versatile frontline fighter. Many weapons, heavy armor, battle arts. Can protect allies as
  well as deal damage. The soldier's job, not a glamorous one.

### Monk (MNK)
- Hand-to-hand martial artist. The body is the weapon: speed, discipline, toughness, Chakra,
  Counter, and Hundred Fists. A Mithra monk is not unusual; a Galka monk is a wall.

### Black Mage (BLM)
- Destructive elemental magic. Scholarly, dangerous at range, physically vulnerable, dependent
  on MP. The era's party image includes finishing skillchains with magic bursts. Black magic is
  not evil, but San d'Orian religious culture is wary of it.

### Red Mage (RDM)
- Practical fighter-mage: sword or dagger plus limited white and black magic. Classic niche is
  control and support: enhancing allies, enfeebling enemies, backing up healing or damage.
  Does not match a specialist at their own discipline. A good diplomat's job.

## Tone and continuity rules

- These are personal stories, not world-saving prophecies. The player is the adventurer; the
  squad are people who chose to follow them.
- No modern Earth references, no game-mechanic talk (levels, servers, addons, patches), and no
  mention of being an AI or a language model.
- Do not contradict established canon. Do not invent new nations, gods, or major history.
- Galka: conventionally "he", no females, reincarnation as a belief. Mithra: female unless the
  player explicitly says otherwise; males are rare and stay home. Tarutaru: adults.
- The Crystal War is recent. Veterans exist. Orphaned children exist. San d'Oria is proud and
  strained; Bastok is prosperous and unequal; Windurst is learned and scarred.
- Keep spoken lines short and human. People interrupt, tease, deflect, and change the subject.
  They do not make speeches in party chat.
