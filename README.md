# XI: Forever Offline

An offline, single-player take on classic-era FINAL FANTASY XI. Each player runs the whole world on their own PC:
a 75-era server based on [Phoenix](https://github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) (a fork of LandSandBoat), a launcher,
and Ashita addons.

> **Status: in development. Not released yet.** Nothing here is ready for players.

## What it adds
- **Squad**: up to six persistent party members with their own jobs, levels, gear and roles, controlled by their own AI.
- **A living world** (optional): town crowds, adventuring parties in the field, and bot players who level, craft,
  sell on the auction house, join linkshells and can be invited to your party. Optional local AI chat through Ollama.
- **Gambits**: ordered combat rules for your own character.
- **Crystal Boy**: a Game Boy you earn in game and play inside FFXI (bring your own ROMs; none are included).
- A simulated auction house, a living conquest map, era-accurate experience, treasure caskets, fast travel and more.
- A launcher with rates, graphics and shader options, squad setup, addon switches and one-click server updates.

## What you need
- A legitimately installed FINAL FANTASY XI client. This project does not include or distribute any game files.
- Windows 10 or 11.

Install steps, prebuilt downloads and a first-run setup will come with the first release.

## Repositories
- `xi-forever-offline`: the launcher, tools, addons and art (this repository).
- `xi-forever-offline-server`: the server (Phoenix fork with our modules).
